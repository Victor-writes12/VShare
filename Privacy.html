Privacy Policy

Last updated, August 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how VSHARE T&T (VSHARE Transport & Trucking, referred to as "VSHARE T&T", "the Company", "We", "Us" or "Our") collects, uses and discloses information when you use our website and booking platform, and explains the privacy choices available to you.

VSHARE T&T operates this website to plan, coordinate and track freight, trucking and delivery services for our customers, and to run VSHARE T&T as a commercial transportation business. We collect only the information reasonably needed to provide the service you request, process your quote or booking, and operate our business responsibly and profitably.

1. Definitions

Words with capitalised first letters carry the meanings set out below, whether used in the singular or the plural.

  • Company refers to Vshare Limited, Ajah, Lekki, Lagos, Nigeria, trading as VSHARE T&T.
  • Service refers to the VSHARE T&T website and any booking, quote or tracking tools available on it.
  • Personal Data means any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual, and is used interchangeably with "Personal Information" in this policy.
  • Usage Data means data collected automatically from your use of the Service or from the Service infrastructure itself, such as the pages you visit and the time spent on them.
  • Cookies are small files placed on your device by a website, used to remember details of your visit.
  • Service Provider means any company or individual VSHARE T&T engages to help operate the Service, such as hosting, email or analytics providers.
  • You means the individual, or the company or entity on whose behalf that individual is acting, who accesses or uses the Service.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Data You Provide

While using the Service, we may ask you to provide certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you, including but not limited to:

  • Email address, first name and last name
  • Phone number
  • Company name, pickup address and destination address
  • Cargo description, declared value, weight and dimensions submitted with a quote request

Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically when you use the Service. This may include your device's IP address, browser type and version, the pages of our Service that you visit, the time and date of your visit, time spent on those pages, unique device identifiers and other diagnostic data. When you access the Service through a mobile device, we may also collect your mobile device type, unique device ID and mobile operating system.

3. Cookies And Tracking Technology

We use cookies and similar tracking technology to operate and improve the Service. A cookie is a small file placed on your device; you can set your browser to refuse cookies or alert you when a cookie is sent, though some parts of the Service may not work correctly without them.

Necessary Cookies
Session cookies administered by us, essential for the booking and quote tools to function correctly.
Preference Cookies
Persistent cookies that remember choices you make on the website, such as a saved tracking number or form entry, so you do not have to re enter them.
Analytics Cookies
Help us understand how visitors use the Service, so we can improve routes, response times and site performance.

Where required by applicable law, we use non essential cookies only with your consent, which you may withdraw at any time through your browser or device settings.

4. How We Use Your Information

VSHARE T&T uses the information we collect to run our logistics business and to keep you informed about your shipment. This includes:

  • Processing quote requests, confirming bookings and scheduling pickups
  • Coordinating pickup, transit and delivery through our fleet and partner network
  • Providing shipment status updates through our tracking tool
  • Contacting you by email, phone or SMS about a booking, delivery update or support request
  • Processing payment for services rendered and maintaining accurate business records
  • Sending service news and offers, where you have subscribed or otherwise agreed to receive them
  • Analysing usage trends to improve our routes, pricing and overall service quality

5. How We Share Information

VSHARE T&T does not sell your personal information. We share information only where necessary to deliver our services, run our business or meet a legal obligation, including with:

  • Drivers and partner fleet operators, who need pickup and delivery details to complete a shipment
  • Service Providers, such as hosting, email, payment and analytics partners who support our booking and tracking systems
  • Affiliates, where applicable, who are required to honour this Privacy Policy
  • Customs and regulatory authorities, where required for cross border freight
  • A buyer or successor, in the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of some or all of VSHARE T&T's business assets
  • Law enforcement or public authorities, where required by law or in response to a valid legal request

6. Text Message Notice

If you opt in to receive text (SMS) messages from us, we will collect and store the phone number you provide, the date and method of your consent, and message delivery information. We use text messages to send delivery notifications, booking updates, one time passwords, security alerts and, where you have agreed to receive them, promotional offers.

Your mobile information is never shared with or sold to third parties for marketing purposes; it is only shared with the Service Providers technically required to deliver the messages. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or reply HELP for support. Message and data rates may apply.

7. Retention Of Your Information

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, comply with our legal and tax obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. Where possible, we apply shorter retention periods or reduce identifiability by deleting or anonymising data once it is no longer needed.

  • Booking and shipment records, retained for the duration needed to complete the delivery, plus a reasonable period afterward for accounting and dispute resolution
  • Customer support correspondence, retained to resolve follow up enquiries and track service quality
  • Website usage data and server logs, retained for security monitoring and troubleshooting

You may request information about how long we retain a specific category of your data by contacting us using the details below.

8. Transfer Of Your Information

Your information is processed at VSHARE T&T's operating offices in Nigeria and, where a Service Provider is located elsewhere, may be transferred to and stored in that location. Where required by applicable law, we take reasonable steps to ensure such transfers are subject to appropriate safeguards and that your data continues to be treated securely.

9. Your Rights And Choices

You have the right to access, correct or request deletion of the personal information we hold about you, and to unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link included in those messages. Please note that we may need to retain certain records, such as completed shipment history, where we have a legal or accounting obligation to do so.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details at the bottom of this page.

10. Legal Disclosure

VSHARE T&T may disclose your personal information where we believe, in good faith, that doing so is necessary to comply with a legal obligation, protect the rights or property of VSHARE T&T, investigate possible wrongdoing connected with the Service, protect the personal safety of our users, drivers or the public, or defend against legal liability.

11. Security Of Your Information

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we hold, including restricted access to booking and shipment systems. No method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, so while we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

12. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for business and adult use and is not directed at anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

13. Links To Other Websites

Our Service may contain links to third party websites that are not operated by VSHARE T&T. We have no control over, and assume no responsibility for, the content or privacy practices of any third party site, and we encourage you to review the privacy policy of every site you visit.

14. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services or in applicable law. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the "Last updated" date above; where changes are significant, we will also notify you by email or through a notice on the Service.
