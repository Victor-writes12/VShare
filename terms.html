1. Nature Of Our Services

VSHARE T&T plans, coordinates and tracks freight transportation using a hybrid network of owned assets and verified third party fleet owners, including vans, cargo trucks and heavy duty vehicles. When you book a vehicle through VSHARE T&T, you authorise us to manage dispatch, routing and delivery fulfilment through our designated drivers or partner network.

2. Customer Obligations And Declared Value

Before any shipment moves through our network, the customer must:

  • Provide accurate descriptions of the cargo, weight, dimensions, pickup location and destination
  • Explicitly declare the true monetary value of the goods prior to dispatch
  • Ensure that items are properly packaged and secured to withstand standard transit conditions on Nigerian roads

3. Prohibited And Restricted Items Policy

Critical Security Warning

VSHARE T&T strictly prohibits the shipment of illegal substances, narcotics, firearms, ammunition, explosives, hazardous or corrosive chemicals, raw currency and any goods classified as contraband under the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

VSHARE T&T and its drivers reserve the absolute right to inspect packages before loading. Any attempt to ship prohibited items will result in immediate cancellation of service without refund, forfeiture of goods and formal reporting to law enforcement agencies, including the NDLEA, EFCC or Nigeria Police Force.

4. Limitation Of Liability And Insurance Cap

While we thoroughly vet all transit vehicles and experienced drivers, VSHARE T&T operates within complex local transport environments.

  • VSHARE T&T shall not be held liable for loss, damage or delivery delays resulting from events beyond our reasonable control, known as Force Majeure. This includes, but is not limited to, traffic accidents, civil unrest, severe weather, flooding, structural road failures or unlawful third party interferences.
  • In the event of proven operational negligence resulting in physical loss or damage, VSHARE T&T's maximum financial liability or payout is strictly capped at ₦50,000, or the total cost of the delivery fee paid for that specific trip, whichever is lower, unless the customer purchased explicit premium cargo insurance through us prior to pickup.

5. Waiting Time, Loading And Demurrage

To keep our connected city and regional networks running efficiently, time limits are enforced at pickup and drop off zones.

Delivery Vans And Small Trucks
Allowed a maximum of 45 minutes for loading and 45 minutes for offloading.
Heavy Duty Trucks, Up To 30 Tonnes
Allowed a maximum of 2 hours for loading and 2 hours for offloading.

Delays exceeding these allocated time windows due to client unreadiness will attract an automatic waiting surcharge of ₦5,000 per hour, which must be cleared before cargo release.

6. Pricing, Payments And Geographic Surcharges

All quotes generated through the VSHARE T&T platform are based on dynamic distance calculations, vehicle selection and specific operational zones. Standard quotes apply strictly to accessible commercial routes; moving items into heavily congested port corridors such as Apapa, deep outskirts or difficult unpaved terrain will trigger situational geographic surcharges to cover vehicle wear and severe traffic delays.

7. Cancellations And Rescheduling

You may cancel or reschedule a booking before a vehicle has been dispatched to your pickup location at no charge. Once a vehicle has been dispatched, a cancellation may attract a dispatch fee to cover the driver's time and mileage; our support team will confirm this fee, where applicable, before your cancellation is processed.

8. Governing Law

These Terms and Conditions are governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Any disputes arising from services rendered shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in Lagos State.
